# Store knowledge

Extra answers for topics your Shopify store has no policy page for. With a real store
(`SHOPIFY_STORE_DOMAIN`), the assistant reads shipping, returns, payment and contact from
Shopify's own policy pages, and everything else from the `##` sections below.
Section titles map to topics: Shipping, Returns, Payment, Warranty, Size guide, Contact, Discounts.

## Warranty
All products have a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Write to us with your
order number and a photo, and we repair, replace or refund the item.

## Size guide
Tops and jackets by chest circumference: S 88-94 cm, M 94-100 cm, L 100-106 cm, XL 106-112 cm.
Between two sizes? Take the larger one for jackets and the smaller one for base layers.

## Discounts
Newsletter subscribers get 10% off their first order. Discount codes can't be combined.
